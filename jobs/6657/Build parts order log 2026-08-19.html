Next five off the quote, in the order I would pull them

Ranked by how soon the window closes on installing them, not by cost. Everything here is already on the 73‑line tech list, so none of it moves the quoted number.

  Part number Description Qty Why now
1 1962429
1515925		 Jet, piston cooling
Bolt		 6
6		 The cooling jets bolt into the block and spray the underside of the pistons. They have to be in before a single piston goes down a bore. Liners are already in, so pistons are the next thing to happen. Miss these and you pull every piston back out. The bolts are the matching hardware and they get torqued and often secured, so do not reuse the old ones.
2 3483295 Liner o‑ring kit 6 Liner projection gets set dry with the shims, then the liners come back out, the o‑rings and crevice seal go on, and they get seated for final. If the liners in line 1 are only dry‑set right now, this is the part that lets you finish that step. Nothing above the block can be trusted until the liners are sealed and re‑checked.
3 6060909 Bronze overhaul kit 1 This is the gasket and seal pack for the whole job and it is the single biggest unknown on the build. The spacer plate gasket almost certainly lives in here. Get it in hand and physically inventory it against what the plate needs before the plate goes on, because if the gasket is not in the kit you are ordering it and waiting while the engine sits open.
4 7F8817 Dowel 2 The spacer plate locates on the block dowels. They go in with the plate, and they are a two dollar part that stops a very expensive alignment problem. Since the plate is already inbound, these should be in the same box, not ordered after you find out the old ones are damaged.
5 10R7155 Camshaft 1 Cam‑in‑block engine, so the cam goes in before the head, the valve train and the injectors. It is also a reman with a core and one of the longest lead times on the list. Order it now even though it installs later, because it is the part most likely to stall the build if you wait until you need it.

Sixth, and it is close

Crank seals 4W8133 front and 4W8134 rear, plus 9N0037 carrier seals x2 and the 1121102 o‑ring seals. These install while the block is still open at both ends. Once the flywheel housing, front cover and pan close up, the window shuts. If the bottom end is going together in the next stretch, promote these ahead of the camshaft.

Open items on this build

Pistons and rings are not accounted for

The quote priced 20R0630 reman cylinder packs, which come as an assembly: liner, piston, rings and pin. Line 1 replaced that with bare 5560701 liners. Unless the original pistons are being cleaned and reused with new rings, pistons and rings need their own part numbers on this log and their own money. This is the biggest open question on the build and it needs answering before the short block closes up.

Thrust bearing

5L1100 thrust bearing and 4N8626 races x2 are on the quote. If the block came back with the crank already in, confirm these went in at the machine shop and log them. If the crank is still out, they are ahead of everything in the list above.

Cost drift

The quoted basket was $22,869.75 at Peterbilt for all 73 tech lines with cores returned. Every row tagged Added or Substitution moves that number. Keep the tags honest and the delta stays visible when the job gets invoiced.

Chassis side, not on any engine parts list

From the Kustom Truck packet in this folder (Peterbilt NAMUX3 repower). None of this is on the 73‑line tech sheet, and none of it is quoted. It is a separate work‑stream from the engine build and it has its own parts and its own software.

Part / item What it is Why it matters
19906013
19905013		 Power Fuse Harness
Power Adapter Harness		 Kustom Truck expects one or the other to feed the J142 fuse harness connector, which carries the main ECM unswitched battery power. Neither is on the engine quote. If neither was bought, the J142 has to be landed on a fused constant source, 15 amp on each wire.
Bendix
EC60 / EC80		 ABS module Retarder Control and ATC Control have to be disabled. On Advanced or Premium modules those are hard coded and cannot be changed, and the module must be replaced with a Basic module to clear the codes. That is a surprise part if nobody checks which module the truck has before the swap.
Software Paccar ESA
Bendix ACOM		 ESA is needed in the CECU to disable "Use Outside Air Temp From Engine Message" or the dash OAT reads wrong. ACOM is needed for the ABS self‑config and parameters. Confirm both are on hand before the truck goes back together.
Doc 9025 TPS sheet, Cummins CM870 ECM Open question. The throttle position sheet in the packet is written for a Cummins CM870. A Cat C18 runs a Cat ADEM, not a CM870. Either Kustom Truck included a generic sheet, or the harness kit that was ordered is spec'd for a Cummins repower. Worth one phone call before the harness goes in.

How to keep this current

  • One row per part number, in the order it was ordered, not the order it was installed.
  • Vs. quote is one of three: On quote it was on the 73‑line tech list, Substitution it replaced a quoted line, Added it was never quoted.
  • Status moves On order to Received to Installed.
  • Note the vendor and the PO or quote number in Notes once parts start coming from more than one place.
